### Netlify’s free plan FAQs
####  Can I set a hard spending limit and have it shut off? 
The free plan is always free, with hard monthly limits that cannot be exceeded or incur any costs. Paid plans have spend control using the auto recharge setting for credits, which automatically refills your credit balance in small increments based on your plan.
Auto recharge is turned off by default so you just need to keep it off. Only Team Owners can enable auto recharge.
####  Are new projects public as soon as I deploy? 
No. On new credit-based pricing accounts, every new project starts private by default. You can build, review, and refine your work without making it public, then publish it when you’re ready.
### Netlify credits FAQs
####  How do credits work for bandwidth, compute, AI inference, web requests, and production deploys? 
Each metered feature consumes a certain number of credits based on how much your projects use.
Learn more about how each feature or meter is measured using credits in our [How credits work](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/how-credits-work/) docs.
####  What happens when a project reaches its credit limit? 
When a site reaches its monthly usage limits, it enters a paused state until the start of the next billing cycle. We’ll notify you by email and in-app as you approach your limits at 50%, 75%, and 100% of usage. Please note that if one project exceeds its limits, all projects on your account will be paused.
You can re-enable your sites at any time by upgrading your plan. Your projects will automatically resume normal operation after upgrading.
####  How do the Pro plan monthly credit tiers work? 
On the Pro plan, Team Owners can choose a monthly credit tier that fits their usage or expected seasonal surges, from 3,000 up to 20,000 credits, for a single flat monthly price. Tiers above 3,000 credits also roll over unused monthly credits as long as you keep your pricing plan tier. These rollovers stay available for one additional billing cycle before they expire. The base 3,000-credit tier doesn’t support rollovers.
You can change your tier at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and reset your billing cycle, while downgrades are scheduled to take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. Credits from credit packs or auto recharge aren’t affected. Learn more in our [Pro plan docs](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/credit-based-pricing-plans/#pro-plan).
### Billing and teams FAQs
####  How does billing work for metered features and extra usage packages? 
You can enable auto recharge for projects on your team, which ensures they stay active and can receive web requests, and more.
Learn more about how you can buy more credits when you run out in smaller batches with [auto recharge](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/configure-auto-recharge/).
####  How does pricing work for adding people to my Netlify team? 
If you have a Free or Personal plan, you cannot add any additional team member seats.
As of April 14th, credit-based Pro plans no longer charge for any seat. With any plan, you can add unlimited free Reviewer roles to your team to give people a Netlify login that they can use to review your team’s web projects and share feedback.
Learn more about [how roles and permissions work](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/team-management/roles-and-permissions/).
####  Can I pay with PayPal, wire transfer, crypto, or any method other than credit card? 
Netlify only accepts credit cards unless you have a Netlify Enterprise plan, in which case we do accept payment via ACH or wire transfer.
####  Do you offer discounts for open source projects? 
Of course. Our [Netlify Open Source plan](https://www.netlify.com/open-source) includes generous monthly credits, free production deploys, and unlimited team members for qualifying projects.
### Existing customer FAQs
####  I’m an existing customer, do I have to change plans? 
No, if you created a Netlify account before September 4, 2025, you can keep your current plan. No changes needed.
If you want to migrate to the new plans and are a [Team Owner](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/team-management/roles-and-permissions/#owner), go to [Usage & billing > Plan details](https://app.netlify.com/teams/~/billing/general#plan-details) and select the **Change team plan** button to choose your new plan.
