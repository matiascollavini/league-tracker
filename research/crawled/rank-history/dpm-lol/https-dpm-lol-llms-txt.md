# dpm.lol
 
> League of Legends statistics, builds, tier lists, matchups, and analytics platform.
 
DPM.LOL provides data-driven League of Legends insights including champion builds, runes, items, win rates, tier lists, player profiles, pro player stats, leaderboards, and matchup videos. Data is updated every patch from millions of ranked games across all regions. For esports content (pick'ems, news, forums, community ratings), see our sister platform RFT.GG.
 
URL: https://dpm.lol
Sitemap: https://dpm.lol/sitemap-all.xml
 
## Champion Builds & Stats
 
Optimal builds, runes, skill orders, items, and matchup data for every champion in every role. Updated each patch with data from millions of ranked games.
 
- [All Champions](https://dpm.lol/champions/all): Browse all League of Legends champions with key stats.
- [Champion Build](https://dpm.lol/build/{champion}/{role}): Detailed build page for a specific champion in a specific role (top, jungle, middle, bottom, utility). Includes win rate, pick rate, ban rate, best runes, item builds, skill order, and counters. Example: https://dpm.lol/build/Jinx/bottom
- 835 champion/role build pages available covering every viable champion-role combination.
 
## Tier Lists
 
Data-driven tier lists ranking champions by performance across all roles and ranks.
 
- [Tier List](https://dpm.lol/tier-list/all/emerald/patch): Overall tier list filtered by rank and patch. Champions ranked from S+ to D tier based on win rate, pick rate, and ban rate.
 
## Leaderboards
 
Top players ranked by LP across all major regions (EUW, KR, NA, and more).
 
- [Global Leaderboard](https://dpm.lol/leaderboard/all): Top ranked players across all regions.
- [Champion Leaderboard](https://dpm.lol/leaderboard/champion/{champion}): Best players worldwide on a specific champion. Example: https://dpm.lol/leaderboard/champion/Jinx
- 168 champion-specific leaderboard pages available.
 
## Player Profiles
 
Detailed stats, match history, and performance analytics for any League of Legends summoner.
 
- [Player Profile](https://dpm.lol/{summonerName}): Search any player by summoner name to view their rank, match history, champion pool, win rates, and performance trends.
 
## Pro Players
 
Professional player profiles with solo queue stats, competitive history, and esports performance data.
 
- [Pro Player Profile](https://dpm.lol/pro/{proName}): Detailed pro player page with SoloQ stats and esports data. Example: https://dpm.lol/pro/Faker
 
## Matchups
 
Video-based matchup analysis for champion vs champion lane matchups.
 
- [Matchups](https://dpm.lol/matchups): Browse and search over 30,000 champion matchup videos to learn how to play any lane matchup.
 
## Data Studio
 
Advanced data analysis and visualization tools for deep League of Legends analytics.
 
- [Data Studio](https://dpm.lol/data-studio): Custom data exploration tools for advanced users.
 
## Team SoloQ
 
Team composition builder and analysis for ranked play.
 
- [Team SoloQ](https://dpm.lol/team-soloq): Build and analyze team compositions for solo queue.
 
## RFT.GG — Esports Hub by DPM.LOL
 
All esports content lives on our sister platform RFT.GG.
 
- [RFT.GG](https://rft.gg): Esports pick'ems, news, forums, community ratings, and tournament data — made by DPM.LOL.
