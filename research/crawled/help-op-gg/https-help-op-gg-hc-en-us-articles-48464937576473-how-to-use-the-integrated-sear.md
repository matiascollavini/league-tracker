# searching stats
OP.GG's desktop app offers an 'integrated search feature' that allows users to view the stats of various games such as League of Legends, Teamfight Tactics (TFT), and Valorant from a single search bar.
![15.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48465056985625)
**✅ how to search stats**
  * Click the search bar at the top of the app or in the center of the home screen.
  * Shortcut: Ctrl + Space
  * Enter the **player name + #tag line** in the search bar.  
Example: Hide on bush#KR1
  * Ensure to enter the exact spacing, capitalization, and tag line for accurate search results.


🔍 **information available in search results**
  * Games the player is active in (e.g., League of Legends, TFT, etc.)
  * Server region (KR, NA, EU, etc.)
  * Current tier, level, recent stats, and other key details


Clicking on the search results will lead you to the detailed stats page of that user.
💡
If there are many search results or if you find it difficult to find the desired account, please enter the exact combination of nickname and tag.
Utilizing the integrated search feature allows for rapid checks of activities across various games, aiding in strategic analysis.
# searching settings
The integrated search feature can be used to quickly find the settings menu you want.
![16.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48465011654937)
🔍 **common settings items**
  * Overlay settings
  * Language settings
  * Game data collection settings
  * App size settings, etc.


Clicking on each item takes you directly to the respective menu.
By using the integrated search function, it becomes convenient to quickly access settings without manually searching through menus.  
Apart from settings, it can also be utilized for searching stats, deck combinations, champion analysis, and exploring various other features.
