# What is a Riot ID?
A Riot ID consists of a player name (e.g., OPGG) and tagline (e.g., Hello), and is used across all Riot Games titles.
![1.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48970350404377)
# Where can I find my Riot ID?
A Riot ID can be found in the League of Legends client and in the Riot Account Management page. It can also be quickly checked on OP.GG.
## OP.GG
Use the [Check Riot ID] feature at the top of the dropdown list when you click the search bar. After logging in with Riot, you will be redirected to your Summoner page.
![2.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48970350404889)
### ![3.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48970350405273)
## League of Legends client
You can find your Riot ID in your Summoner profile within the League of Legends client.
![4.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48970350406041)
## Riot Account Management
[Go to the Riot Account Management page](https://account.riotgames.com/) to view or change your Riot ID.
![5.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48970374109593)
# How to search stats using Riot ID
## Web (PC/Mobile)
  1. **Search by player name and tagline**
Select the region and enter both the player name and tagline in the search bar to search.
![6.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48970350413593)
  2. **Search using [Check Riot ID]**
Use the [Check Riot ID] feature at the top of the dropdown list when you click the search bar. After logging in with Riot, you will be redirected to your Summoner page.
![7.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48970350413849)
  3. **Search from [My Profile]**
If logged in with your OP.GG account, the linked Riot account will appear in [My Profile]. Clicking it will take you to the Summoner page for that account.


## Mobile App (Android/iOS)
  1. **Search by player name and tagline**
Select the region and enter both the player name and tagline in the search bar to search.
![8.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48970374111001)
  2. **Link Riot Account**
After logging in with your OP.GG account and linking your Riot account, you can quickly access your Summoner page from the home screen at any time.
![9.png](https://help.op.gg/hc/article_attachments/48970350414745)
