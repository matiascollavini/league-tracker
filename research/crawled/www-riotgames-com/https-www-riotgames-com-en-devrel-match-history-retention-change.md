Hey folks,
Starting on **Wednesday, August 7th, 2019** we’ll be reducing Match History retention from three years to two years on a rolling cadence. This means that data from March of 2018 will be removed during March of 2020, for example. This will not affect timeline data, which will continue to have a one year retention policy. 
If you have any questions or concerns feel free to ask them on the Developer [Discord](http://discord.gg/riotapi) or message us on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/RiotGamesApi)
