Hey guys
During the past few days, I've got multiple reports of spectator endpoint in EUNE returning http404 whereas the player was ingame.
For instance, the player "Valco3" (EUNE) started a game (matchId: 2217846133) on July 5th, 8:28:17AM GMT  
On July 5th, 8:29:26 AM GMT I called <https://eun1.api.riotgames.com/lol/spectator/v4/active-games/by-summoner/theEncryptedSummonerId> and got a http 404
Impact: the players see them as offline on porofessor.gg and cannot see the data for their match. So porofessor is basically useless in that case
Likelihood. Around 15% of players in the related regions (about 6k unique users affected per day). I got a few dozens of reports, probably the highest number of reports on an individual issue
Could you have a look please ?
Thanks :)
